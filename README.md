> **“I’ve seen this person before… but who are they again?”**

NPC (*Need Pamong Context*) is a gamified learning application designed to help Taruna become familiar with the names, faces, and roles of the Satuan Pengasuhan through interactive, memory-based gameplay.

The idea came from a simple problem: recognizing someone is not quite the same as remembering who they are.
NPC turns that awkward *“I know that face”* moment into something a little more playable.

> **Learn the face. Remember the name. Get the context.**

## 🎮 The Idea

Getting familiar with the people around you can be surprisingly difficult when there are a lot of names, faces, and roles to remember at once. NPC provides a small cognitive head start by introducing them through:

**Recognition → Association → Recall → Interaction**

The goal isn't to make Taruna memorize people like a database. It's to make the next encounter feel a little less unfamiliar.

---

## 🧠 Learning by Playing

NPC is designed around the principles of **Cognitive Load Theory**, breaking information into smaller stages rather than presenting everything at once.

Players gradually move from:

**Observe → Recognize → Associate → Retrieve → Apply**

In other words:
> See them.  
> Recognize them.  
> Connect the dots.  
> Remember them.  
> Then go meet them.

---

## 🃏 What's in the Game?

- 👤 **Pamong Recognition** — learn names, faces, and roles through interactive challenges.
- 🧩 **Memory Challenges** — test recognition, recall, and association.
- 🃏 **Collectible Cards** — unlock Pamong cards as you progress.
- 🏆 **Hall of Legends** — see how your score stacks up.
- ❤️ **Revival System** — because one wrong answer shouldn't end the story.
- 🏅 **Milestones & Achievements** — track progress beyond just getting the answers right.
- 🎻 **Bardcore Soundtrack** — because apparently, learning about Pamong is better with medieval music playing in the background.

---

## 🏆 Progression

NPC is built around **progress, not just completion**.
Players earn scores, reach milestones, unlock collectibles, and gradually build familiarity with the Satuan Pengasuhan. The idea is simple:

> **The more you play, the more familiar the faces become.**

---

## 🎯 Why NPC?

Because sometimes the problem isn't:
> *“I don't know this person.”*
It's:
> *“I know I've seen this person somewhere…”*

NPC exists somewhere between **not knowing** and **actually knowing someone**.
It provides the context in between. And that's intentional. The game can introduce a person. It cannot introduce you **to** them.

## 📌 A Small Disclaimer

NPC is an **introduction tool**, not a replacement for real interaction.

The information presented in the game is meant to help with initial recognition and familiarity. A person, fortunately, is more complicated than a profile card.

---

**the role.**
